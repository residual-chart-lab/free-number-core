# Relationship to the frozen Core

The body of this gift remains [Free Numbers Core v1.0.0](https://github.com/residual-chart-lab/free-number-core/releases/tag/core-v1.0.0), commit `9efc241d38e5ca2cd07f425b747bfb5d67ea0235`.
Version DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21328471 . Concept DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21328470 . MIT license.

The frozen Core already proves all-grade response reconstruction and a uniform associative bridge composition:

- [Core 08: all-grade reconstruction](https://github.com/residual-chart-lab/free-number-core/blob/9efc241d38e5ca2cd07f425b747bfb5d67ea0235/core/08-all-grade-response-reconstruction.md)
- [Core 09: closure theorem](https://github.com/residual-chart-lab/free-number-core/blob/9efc241d38e5ca2cd07f425b747bfb5d67ea0235/core/09-core-closure-theorem.md)

The November 6 proofs are a separate companion. T01 reuses the Core's length-two splitting. T03 rederives response reconstruction on quaternion tensor spaces with an explicit normalization, inverse and norm statement; its imaginary-gap supplement gives another recursive formulation and bounds. **Reconstruction in every finite grade is already a Core result; this gift does not count that fact as a new discovery.**

The spectral transport, rank-one classification, spin identities and prescribed-background KK reduction are stated with their own hypotheses. Standard constructions and rederivations are identified in `proofs/SOURCES.json` and the texts. These are 16 labeled claims plus two supplements, not a claim of 18 novel theorems.

The November 6 source record originally cited Core note 04. The public comparison above also checks Core notes 08 and 09. Audited proof files and calculation sources are preserved byte for byte; this attribution clarification is a publication wrapper, not an unreported change to those proofs.

The applications act on fixed Core coordinates from outside the Core. Prescribed SU(2) graph transport and conditional transport generated from residual responses are separate examples. No new Core version or alteration of the frozen tag is made.
